# Why the phone leaves the bedroom

Sources: D1 `a-newsletter.md` “Why the phone leaves the bedroom”; D2 `b-talk.md` “Attention is an environment”; D3 `c-notes.md` “Chapter notes”.

## Willpower is the wrong lever

Focus is not a character trait. It is a room. Environment design beats willpower. Put the charger where the morning does not start with a screen.

— D1-S1-P1; D2-S1-P1; D3-S1-P1; D2-S2-P1; D1-S1-P2; D2-S2-P2

## The eight-second claim

Popular talks still quote that average focus lasts 8 seconds [1]. That number is a staging device, not a diagnosis.

— D1-S2-P1; D1-S2-P2; D2-S3-P1; D2-S3-P2; D3-S2-P1; D3-S2-P2; D1-R1

### Extra source

A 2024 methods note warns that the 8-second figure is recycled from an unreviewed slide deck [2].

## A working change

A client moved the phone charger to the kitchen. The first week they woke without opening a feed.
